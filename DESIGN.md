## Overview
A professional, grounded, and optimistic internal knowledge hub for Moss Business Analysts. The interface prioritizes clear paths to trusted resources and practical learning.

## Color usage
Logo Green is the dominant action and navigation color. Gold Brass appears on every screen as a restrained accent. Sand Dune supports page backgrounds, while white surfaces and Everglade tints create hierarchy. Coral and Lime Green are reserved for status and emphasis.

## Typography
Arial is used throughout. Page titles use bold Title Case; small labels may use uppercase with modest tracking. Body copy is sentence case and concise.

## Layout
A persistent 248px left navigation anchors the desktop experience. Main content uses a generous 32–40px rhythm, clear cards, and content-first grids. Borders and selective shadows provide depth without visual noise.

## Do's and Don'ts
- Do: Keep navigation stable, labels plain, and primary actions unmistakably green.
- Do: Use Gold Brass as a small accent on every screen.
- Do: Reflect Moss values through helpful, people-first copy.
- Don't: Use blue, purple, generic red, decorative fonts, or gradients.
- Don't: Crowd cards or rely on shadows for every surface.
