Stakeholder Analysis Accelerator

A repeatable workflow that combines MossAIc with proven BA practice to map who matters on your initiative — their influence, needs, risks, and the best way to engage each one.

15 min

Step-by-Step Workflow

Work through each phase in order. Keep human judgment in the loop at every step.

1

List your stakeholders

Start from what you know and let MossAIc help you find gaps.

  • Brain-dump every person, role, and group touched by the initiative.
  • Prompt: 'Given this project description, what stakeholder roles am I likely missing?'
  • Add any suggested roles that genuinely apply.
2

Assess influence and interest

Position each stakeholder so you can prioritize your attention.

  • Rate each stakeholder on influence (high/low) and interest (high/low).
  • Prompt MossAIc to place them on a power/interest grid and explain each placement.
  • Adjust placements using your real-world knowledge of the people involved.
3

Surface needs and risks

Understand what each stakeholder cares about and what could go wrong.

  • For each high-influence stakeholder, capture goals, concerns, and success measures.
  • Prompt: 'What risks or resistance might each of these stakeholders raise?'
  • Confirm anything sensitive directly rather than assuming.
4

Plan engagement

Decide how and how often you'll work with each stakeholder.

  • Ask MossAIc to recommend an engagement approach and cadence per quadrant.
  • Turn the output into a simple stakeholder engagement plan.
  • Review the plan with your practice partner before acting on it.

What You Need

  • A short project description
  • An initial list of known stakeholders
  • Your own knowledge of the people and politics

What You Get

  • A power/interest stakeholder grid
  • Needs, concerns, and risks per stakeholder
  • An engagement plan with cadence
