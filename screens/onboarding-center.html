Who We Are

A people-centered model grounded in relationships

Moss was founded in 2003 by Bob, Chad, and Scott Moss on a people-centered model grounded in relationships. What began as the “Cult of Bob” has grown into the “Culture of Moss” — the same care for people supported by scaled systems, structure, and transparency. Our mantra, “Moss-first, people always,” still guides how we operate every day.

Moss runs two primary businesses: Construction Management (regional) and Utility-Scale Solar EPC (national). Shared Services — Strategy, HR, Finance, IT, Legal, and Business Development — act as enablers across both, and the Vision Leadership Team drives enterprise alignment, trust, and healthy conflict. As a BA, you may support any of these groups depending on where you are assigned.

The Business Analyst Role at Moss

Connecting people, process, and data

At Moss, Business Analysts connect people, process, and data. You'll spend your time understanding how work actually gets done in the field and across shared services, then translating what you learn into clearer processes, better requirements, and decisions our teams can trust. Because we lead from the field, the best BA work here starts with listening to the people closest to the work. Clear analysis is a form of Clear is Kind: it gives leaders and field teams what they need to make good decisions quickly.

Focus AreaWhat It Looks Like at Moss
Process discoveryMap how work flows across the field, projects, and shared services, and find where it breaks down.
Requirements gatheringTranslate stakeholder needs into clear, testable requirements for systems and process changes.
Data and reportingAnalyze operational data to support forecasting, cost control, and performance visibility.
Solution supportHelp evaluate, configure, and roll out tools that make teams more effective.
Change enablementSupport adoption by communicating clearly and closing the loop with stakeholders.
