Safety Is a Promise, Not a Priority

Safety is not one priority among many; it is a promise we keep. Every person at Moss is empowered to stop work for safety, and psychological safety is part of total safety. As a BA, respect this in everything you analyze: never recommend a change that trades safety for speed or cost.

Communication Expectations

Share early, speak plainly, and answer honestly. Clear is kind. Escalate issues early, close loops, and document decisions. Aim to finish every engagement better friends than when you started.
