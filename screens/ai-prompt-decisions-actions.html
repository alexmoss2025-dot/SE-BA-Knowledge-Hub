Meetings

Structure Decisions and Actions

Use this prompt to turn raw meeting notes into a clean, structured record. MossAIc extracts decisions, owners, due dates, risks, open questions, and follow-ups — so nothing falls through the cracks after the meeting ends.

How to Use This Prompt

1

Capture your notes

During or after the meeting, jot down notes in whatever format works — bullet points, free text, or even a transcript.

2

Remove confidential details

Before pasting into MossAIc, redact any sensitive information (personal data, financials, NDA-covered content).

3

Run the prompt

Paste the prompt below into MossAIc along with your meeting notes.

4

Distribute the output

Review the structured output, make corrections, and share with attendees for confirmation.

The Prompt

You are an experienced Business Analyst processing meeting notes.

Meeting notes:
[Paste your raw meeting notes here]

Extract and organize the following into a clean, structured format:

1. **Decisions Made** — Each decision with a brief rationale
2. **Action Items** — Each with: description, owner, due date (or "TBD" if not stated)
3. **Risks Identified** — Any risks, concerns, or blockers mentioned
4. **Open Questions** — Unresolved items that need follow-up
5. **Parking Lot** — Topics raised but deferred to a future discussion
6. **Key Discussion Points** — Brief summary of major topics covered

Use a table for action items. Flag any decisions that seem to lack an owner or where the notes are ambiguous.

When to Use

  • You have rough meeting notes and need to extract clear outcomes
  • You want to ensure every decision has an owner and due date
  • You need to surface risks, open questions, and follow-ups quickly

Pro Tips

  • Run this immediately after the meeting while context is fresh
  • If using a transcript, trim filler and off-topic sections first for cleaner results
  • Pair with your team's standard meeting-minutes template for consistency

Remember

Always remove confidential details before prompting. Review every AI output — you own the final deliverable.
