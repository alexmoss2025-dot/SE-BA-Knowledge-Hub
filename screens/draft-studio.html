FR-01

The system shall allow a Superintendent to create one daily log per project per calendar day, capturing crews on site, headcount, weather, deliveries, and safety observations.

Source: “…write down crews on site, headcount, weather, deliveries, any safety stuff.”

MustHigh
FR-02

The system shall automatically populate weather conditions and hours of weather delay for the jobsite location, with the ability for the Superintendent to adjust and annotate.

Source: “I’d want it to just pull the weather in.”

MustHigh
FR-03

The system shall allow Subcontractor representatives to submit their own crew headcount from a mobile device, held in a Pending state until approved by the Superintendent.

Source: “Subs should be able to add their own headcount… I have to approve it before it counts.”

MustHigh
FR-04

The system shall notify the Safety team of any log entry flagged as a safety incident on the same day it is recorded.

Source: “Safety wants the incident notes same day.”

ShouldMedium
FR-05

The system shall make approved daily logs available to the Project Manager for owner billing review, including a searchable history by date.

Source: “The PM checks it for owner billing… legal digs through old photos.”

MustMedium
NFR-01

The mobile application shall support full log entry while offline and synchronize automatically when connectivity is restored.

Source: “It has to work offline. Half the site has no signal below grade.”

MustHigh
NFR-02

Daily logs shall be retained in an immutable, timestamped record suitable for claims support.

Source: Inferred from “if there’s a claim later…” — confirm retention period with Legal.

ShouldLow

Open questions

Things the notes did not answer. Take these to your next session.

  • What is the cutoff for a log to count as “same day”? Midnight, or end of shift?
  • Can a Superintendent edit a log after approval, or is a correction entry required?
  • Which subcontractors get accounts — every trade, or only those billing by headcount?
  • Retention period for logs used in claims — confirm with Legal.

Assumptions made

  • One Superintendent owns the log per project per day (not confirmed for multi-tower sites).
  • Weather source accuracy is acceptable for contractual time-extension support.
