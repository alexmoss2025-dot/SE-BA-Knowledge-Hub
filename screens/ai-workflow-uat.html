UAT Scenario Builder

Draft business-focused user acceptance test scenarios directly from approved requirements, so testing validates real outcomes rather than just checking boxes.

15 min

Step-by-Step Workflow

Work through each phase in order. Keep human judgment in the loop at every step.

1

Provide approved requirements

Start only from requirements that are signed off.

  • Paste the approved requirements or user stories into MossAIc.
  • Confirm they are the current, approved versions.
  • Note any acceptance criteria already defined.
2

Generate happy-path scenarios

Cover the core business outcomes first.

  • Prompt: 'Write UAT scenarios in Given/When/Then form for the main success paths.'
  • Check each scenario ties back to a specific requirement.
  • Rewrite anything too technical into business language.
3

Add edge cases and negatives

Test the boundaries and error handling.

  • Ask MossAIc for boundary, exception, and negative-path scenarios.
  • Prioritize the ones with real business impact.
  • Discard scenarios that test system internals rather than business behavior.
4

Package for testers

Make the scenarios ready to execute.

  • Ask for a table with columns: Scenario | Steps | Expected Result.
  • Review coverage against the full requirement set.
  • Share with business testers and capture their feedback.

What You Need

  • Approved requirements or user stories
  • Any existing acceptance criteria
  • A sense of real business priorities

What You Get

  • Given/When/Then happy-path scenarios
  • Edge-case and negative scenarios
  • A tester-ready scenario table
