Requirements Change Control

How changes to already-approved requirements are requested, assessed for impact, approved or rejected, and documented to maintain traceability and prevent scope creep.

Back to Library

Overview

This map illustrates the lightweight change control process that governs modifications to baseline requirements after stakeholder approval. It ensures every change is evaluated for impact on scope, schedule, budget, and risk before being accepted — preventing uncontrolled scope creep while remaining agile enough to accommodate legitimate business needs.

Use this map to train project teams on change management, explain to stakeholders why a requested change requires approval, or design process improvements for faster turnaround on low-risk changes.

Key Roles

Change Requestor

Submits the change request, explains the business justification, and clarifies the desired outcome.

Business Analyst

Logs the request, conducts impact analysis, determines approval path, and updates all documentation.

Project Sponsor

Approves or rejects medium-impact changes and attends the Change Control Board for high-impact items.

Change Control Board (CCB)

Cross-functional group (sponsor, PM, technical lead, BA) that decides on high-impact changes based on portfolio fit and resource availability.

Project Manager

Assesses schedule and budget impact, attends CCB meetings, and updates the project plan after approved changes.

Technical Lead

Evaluates technical feasibility, effort, and risk for proposed changes.
