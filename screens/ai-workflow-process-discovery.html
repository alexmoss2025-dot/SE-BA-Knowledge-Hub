Process Discovery Synthesizer

Process Discovery Synthesizer

Turn scattered interview notes and observations into a structured view of a business process — its steps, actors, systems, and gaps — ready to hand off to process mapping.

20 min

Step-by-Step Workflow

Work through each phase in order. Keep human judgment in the loop at every step.

1

Gather your raw inputs

Pull together everything you've captured about the process.

  • Collect interview notes, screenshots, and any existing documentation.
  • Remove confidential or client-identifying details before prompting.
  • Paste the cleaned notes into MossAIc.
2

Extract the process skeleton

Get a first-pass sequence of steps.

  • Prompt: 'From these notes, list the process steps in order, with the actor and system for each.'
  • Review the sequence for missing or out-of-order steps.
  • Correct anything the notes didn't make clear.
3

Identify actors, systems, and handoffs

Make the moving parts explicit.

  • Ask MossAIc to list every actor, system, and handoff point it detected.
  • Flag handoffs where ownership or timing is unclear.
  • Note where data is created, changed, or consumed.
4

Highlight gaps and pain points

Prepare a clean input for process mapping.

  • Prompt: 'Where are the gaps, delays, rework loops, or risks in this process?'
  • Confirm the flagged gaps with the process owner.
  • Export the structured summary to build your process map.

What You Need

  • Interview notes or observations
  • Any existing process documentation
  • Confidential details removed

What You Get

  • An ordered list of process steps
  • Actors, systems, and handoff points
  • A list of gaps and pain points to map
