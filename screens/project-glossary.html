30% Drawing SetThis design milestone aims to layout major design elements of the project. This is the foundation on which the project design will sit.Company-Specific
60% Drawing SetAfter the 30% drawing set is reviewed, the incorporated revisions are published in the 60% design set. At the 60% design phase, the team should focus on reviewing for constructability and value engineering since making changes at this stage is less costly.Company-Specific
90% Drawing SetRevisions made during the 60% design review are incorporated in the 90% design. The 90% drawing set includes all the plans and specifications for the project. Once the review and revisions are made to the 90% set, then the drawings are issued for permitting with the local AHJ.Company-Specific
Acceptance CriteriaSpecific conditions that must be met for a requirement, user story, or solution to be considered complete and acceptable by stakeholders.Business Analyst
ADKAR ModelA goal-oriented change management model that stands for Awareness, Desire, Knowledge, Ability, and Reinforcement.Business Analyst
AgileAn iterative project delivery methodology focused on collaboration, customer feedback, and incremental delivery of value.Business Analyst
ALMApplication Lifecycle Management.Company-Specific
ApprenticeWorker enrolled in a registered apprenticeship program who is learning a skilled trade through a combination of on-the-job training (OTJ or OJT) and classroom instruction (RTI) under supervision of a journey worker or master craftsperson.Company-Specific
ArrayA group of solar panels wired together to create an electrical power output.Company-Specific
As-Built DrawingsThe final drawings and final technical specifications for the work performed, as revised to reflect changes made during construction, and include with reasonable detail the physical placement of equipment, underground structures such as buried piping and utilities, electric one lines, electric schematics and connection diagrams.Company-Specific
AssumptionA factor considered true, real, or certain without proof for planning purposes.Business Analyst
BacklogA prioritized queue of upcoming features, user stories, and tasks managed by the team.Business Analyst
Basis of BidA document that includes the preliminary site design and outlines the qualifications and assumptions that the design and bid was based on.Company-Specific
Basis of DesignA document formed from the Basis of Bid (BoB) and includes comments made by client and forms the foundation of the detail design and engineering phase.Company-Specific
Battery Energy Storage System (BESS)A unit that will consist of multiple Battery units, nested within racks internally, to store power for later usage. Power is stored as DC. A BESS system is used to balance the energy grid by storing surplus energy generated by a plant to release to the grid when less power is produced.Company-Specific
Bill of Materials (BOM)A comprehensive list of all the materials or components needed to construct a product or system.Company-Specific
BRDBusiness Requirements Document. A formal document that captures objectives, requirements, scope, assumptions, constraints, and stakeholder needs.Business Analyst

Business CaseA document that justifies a project or initiative by outlining benefits, costs, risks, and expected outcomes.Business Analyst

CABChange Advisory Board.Company-Specific

Change Request (CR)A formal request to modify scope, requirements, functionality, processes, or project deliverables.Business Analyst
Critical PathThe longest sequence of dependent tasks that must finish on time for the project to end.Business Analyst
CulvertA structure, typically a pipe or box, that allows water to flow under a road, or other obstruction, acting as a drain or waterway crossing.Company-Specific
Current StateThe existing business process, workflow, system, or operating model before improvements are implemented.Business Analyst
DefectA flaw or issue in a system that causes expected results to differ from actual results.Business Analyst
DeliverableA tangible output produced during a project, such as a process map, requirements document, or test plan.Business Analyst
DependencyA task, activity, system, or resource that must be completed or available before another activity can proceed.Business Analyst
DiscoveryThe phase in which requirements, business objectives, stakeholders, risks, and opportunities are identified.Business Analyst
End UserAn individual who ultimately uses a system, product, service, or process.Business Analyst
EnhancementA modification that improves functionality, usability, or performance of an existing process or system.Business Analyst
EpicA large body of work that can be broken into multiple user stories or requirements.Business Analyst
FHP IIField Handoff Procedures II Project.Company-Specific
Functional RequirementA requirement that describes what a system or solution must do.Business Analyst
Future StateThe desired business process, workflow, or operating model after improvements are implemented.Business Analyst
Gap AnalysisThe comparison between the current state and future state to identify gaps that must be addressed.Business Analyst
Go-LiveTransition of a solution into production use.Company-Specific
GovernanceThe framework of processes, controls, policies, and decision-making structures used to manage work.Business Analyst
HypercareA period immediately following deployment where additional support and monitoring are provided.Business Analyst
Hypercare (Company)Post-go-live support period focused on stabilization and issue resolution.Company-Specific

IOMIntegrated Opportunity Management.Company-Specific

To qualify for enhanced tax credits or incentives under the IRA, contractors and subcontractors must meet specific labor-related standards.Company-Specific
IssueA problem that currently exists and requires resolution.Business Analyst
JADJoint Application Development. A facilitated workshop used to gather requirements and align stakeholders on project objectives.Business Analyst
KPIKey Performance Indicator. A measurable value used to evaluate performance against business objectives.Business Analyst
Lessons LearnedKnowledge gained from project successes, challenges, and outcomes to improve future initiatives.Business Analyst
MVPMinimum Viable Product. The smallest set of features needed to deliver value and validate a solution.Business Analyst
Non-Functional RequirementA requirement describing how a system performs rather than what it does, such as security, availability, reliability, or performance.Business Analyst
Operational ReadinessThe state of preparedness required for a business to successfully adopt and support a new solution.Business Analyst
Oracle FusionEnterprise resource planning platform used for business operations.Company-Specific
Plan of the Day (POD)A document sent to the client daily that reflects the previous day's actuals and plans the current day's activities.Company-Specific
Process FlowA visual representation of activities, decisions, inputs, outputs, and responsibilities within a process.Business Analyst

Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM)A document used to track requirements throughout the project lifecycle to ensure they are developed, tested, and implemented.Business Analyst

SMESubject Matter Expert. An individual with specialized knowledge of a business process, system, or function.Business Analyst

Swimlane DiagramA process map that visually organizes activities by role, department, or responsibility area.Business Analyst

User StoryAn Agile requirement typically written as: As a [user], I want [capability] so that [benefit].Business Analyst
