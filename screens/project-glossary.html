Term Definition Category
Acceptance Criteria Specific conditions that must be met for a requirement, user story, or solution to be considered complete and acceptable by stakeholders. Business Analyst
Agile An iterative project delivery methodology focused on collaboration, customer feedback, and incremental delivery of value. Business Analyst
ALM Application Lifecycle Management. Company-Specific
Assumption A factor considered true, real, or certain without proof for planning purposes. Business Analyst
Backlog A prioritized queue of upcoming features, user stories, and tasks managed by the team. Business Analyst
BRD Business Requirements Document. A formal document that captures objectives, requirements, scope, assumptions, constraints, and stakeholder needs. Business Analyst
Business Analyst (BA) A professional responsible for understanding business needs, analyzing processes, documenting requirements, and helping deliver solutions that provide business value. Business Analyst
Business Case A document that justifies a project or initiative by outlining benefits, costs, risks, and expected outcomes. Business Analyst
Business Requirement A high-level statement describing what the business needs to achieve. Business Analyst
CAB Change Advisory Board. Company-Specific
CCB Change Control Board. A governance body responsible for reviewing, approving, rejecting, or prioritizing requested changes. Business Analyst
CCB (Company) Change Control Board. Company-Specific
Change Request (CR) A formal request to modify scope, requirements, functionality, processes, or project deliverables. Business Analyst
Critical Path The longest sequence of dependent tasks that must finish on time for the project to end. Business Analyst
Current State The existing business process, workflow, system, or operating model before improvements are implemented. Business Analyst
Defect A flaw or issue in a system that causes expected results to differ from actual results. Business Analyst
Deliverable A tangible output produced during a project, such as a process map, requirements document, or test plan. Business Analyst
Dependency A task, activity, system, or resource that must be completed or available before another activity can proceed. Business Analyst
Discovery The phase in which requirements, business objectives, stakeholders, risks, and opportunities are identified. Business Analyst
End User An individual who ultimately uses a system, product, service, or process. Business Analyst
Enhancement A modification that improves functionality, usability, or performance of an existing process or system. Business Analyst
Epic A large body of work that can be broken into multiple user stories or requirements. Business Analyst
FHP II Field Handoff Procedures II Project. Company-Specific
Functional Requirement A requirement that describes what a system or solution must do. Business Analyst
Future State The desired business process, workflow, or operating model after improvements are implemented. Business Analyst
Gap Analysis The comparison between the current state and future state to identify gaps that must be addressed. Business Analyst
Go-Live Transition of a solution into production use. Company-Specific
Governance The framework of processes, controls, policies, and decision-making structures used to manage work. Business Analyst
Hypercare A period immediately following deployment where additional support and monitoring are provided. Business Analyst
Hypercare (Company) Post-go-live support period focused on stabilization and issue resolution. Company-Specific
Impact Assessment An evaluation of how a proposed change affects people, processes, technology, and operations. Business Analyst
IOM Integrated Opportunity Management. Company-Specific
Issue A problem that currently exists and requires resolution. Business Analyst
JAD Joint Application Development. A facilitated workshop used to gather requirements and align stakeholders on project objectives. Business Analyst
KPI Key Performance Indicator. A measurable value used to evaluate performance against business objectives. Business Analyst
Lessons Learned Knowledge gained from project successes, challenges, and outcomes to improve future initiatives. Business Analyst
MVP Minimum Viable Product. The smallest set of features needed to deliver value and validate a solution. Business Analyst
Non-Functional Requirement A requirement describing how a system performs rather than what it does, such as security, availability, reliability, or performance. Business Analyst
Operational Readiness The state of preparedness required for a business to successfully adopt and support a new solution. Business Analyst
Oracle Fusion Enterprise resource planning platform used for business operations. Company-Specific
Process Flow A visual representation of activities, decisions, inputs, outputs, and responsibilities within a process. Business Analyst
Product Owner The individual responsible for prioritizing and managing a product backlog and maximizing delivered value. Business Analyst
Project Scope The boundaries of a project defining what is included and excluded. Business Analyst
QRG Quick Reference Guide. A concise document providing step-by-step instructions for performing a task or process. Business Analyst
RAID Log A project management document used to track risks, assumptions, issues, and dependencies. Business Analyst
Requirement A documented need, expectation, capability, or condition required to achieve a business objective. Business Analyst
Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM) A document used to track requirements throughout the project lifecycle to ensure they are developed, tested, and implemented. Business Analyst
Risk A potential future event that could negatively impact objectives, timelines, budget, or quality. Business Analyst
Scope Creep The uncontrolled expansion of project scope without proper review, approval, or governance. Business Analyst
SME Subject Matter Expert. An individual with specialized knowledge of a business process, system, or function. Business Analyst
SOP Standard Operating Procedure. A documented set of instructions describing how a process should be performed. Business Analyst
Stakeholder Any individual, group, or organization affected by or involved in a project, process, or solution. Business Analyst
Swimlane Diagram A process map that visually organizes activities by role, department, or responsibility area. Business Analyst
Test Case A documented set of steps, inputs, and expected results used to validate functionality. Business Analyst
Traceability The ability to link requirements to design, development, testing, and deployment activities. Business Analyst
UAT User Acceptance Testing. The process in which business users validate that a solution meets requirements and is ready for production use. Business Analyst
User Story An Agile requirement typically written as: As a [user], I want [capability] so that [benefit]. Business Analyst
Validation Confirmation that a solution meets business needs and requirements. Business Analyst
Verification Confirmation that a solution was developed according to specifications and design requirements. Business Analyst
WBS (Work Breakdown Structure) A hierarchical breakdown of total project work into smaller, manageable pieces. Business Analyst
Workshop A facilitated collaborative session used to gather information, analyze processes, make decisions, or resolve issues. Business Analyst
Workstream Functional area within a project or program. Company-Specific
