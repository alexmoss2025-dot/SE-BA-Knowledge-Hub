Requirements

Challenge Requirement Quality

Use this prompt as a second set of eyes on a draft requirement or user story. MossAIc checks for ambiguity, testability, hidden assumptions, and missing edge cases so you can strengthen the requirement before it reaches development.

The Prompt

Copy this into MossAIc, then replace the bracketed placeholders with your context.

You are a critical reviewer of business requirements. Review the requirement below and challenge its quality.

Requirement / user story:
"""
[paste the requirement or user story here]
"""

Acceptance criteria (if any):
"""
[paste acceptance criteria, or write "none yet"]
"""

Please respond with:
1. Ambiguities — words or phrases open to interpretation, with a suggested clearer wording.
2. Testability — is each statement verifiable? Flag anything that can't be tested.
3. Hidden assumptions — what is being assumed but not stated.
4. Missing edge cases — error paths, boundary conditions, and exceptions not covered.
5. A rewritten version of the requirement that resolves the issues you found.

Be specific and constructive. Do not invent business rules — mark anything uncertain as a question for the business.

How to Use It

Follow these steps to get a dependable result.

01

Paste your draft

Drop the requirement or user story (and any acceptance criteria) into the bracketed sections.

02

Generate the review

Run it in MossAIc and read through each of the five sections it returns.

03

Confirm open questions

Take the flagged assumptions and questions back to your business stakeholders — don't resolve them by guessing.

04

Update the requirement

Fold the confirmed answers into the rewritten version and update your requirements document.

Tips for a Better Result

  • Review requirements in small batches so feedback stays specific.
  • Ask 'rate each requirement 1-5 on testability' for a quick quality scan.
  • Never let the model invent business rules — always verify with the business.

Before You Prompt

  • Remove confidential or client-identifying details.
  • Give clear context and state the output you need.
  • Review and verify every response before using it.
