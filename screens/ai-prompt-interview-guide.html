Discovery

Build an Interview Guide

Use this prompt to turn a discovery objective into a focused, well-sequenced stakeholder interview guide. It produces open questions grouped by theme so you can uncover goals, pain points, decisions, and success measures without leading the interviewee.

How to Use This Prompt

1

Define your objective

Write a clear one-sentence discovery goal. Example: “Understand how the field team currently tracks equipment inspections and where the process breaks down.”

2

List what you already know

Note any context — systems involved, team names, known pain points — so the prompt can tailor questions rather than repeat obvious ground.

3

Run the prompt

Paste the prompt below into MossAIc, fill in the bracketed placeholders, and submit.

4

Review & refine

Check the generated questions. Remove duplicates, reorder for conversation flow, and add follow-ups specific to your stakeholder.

The Prompt

You are an experienced Business Analyst preparing for a stakeholder interview.

Objective: [Describe the discovery goal in one sentence]
Context: [List known systems, teams, pain points, or constraints]
Stakeholder role: [Title and area of responsibility]

Generate a structured interview guide with:
1. Opening questions (rapport and role context)
2. Current-state questions (process, tools, data)
3. Pain-point questions (friction, workarounds, risks)
4. Future-state questions (goals, success measures, priorities)
5. Closing questions (anything missed, next steps)

Use open-ended questions. Avoid leading or yes/no questions. Group by theme and include 3-5 questions per section.

When to Use

  • You are preparing for a stakeholder interview and need focused questions
  • You want to ensure consistent coverage across multiple interviews
  • You need to uncover goals, pain points, and success measures systematically

Pro Tips

  • Swap in the stakeholder's actual title for more relevant questions
  • Run it once per stakeholder role to create tailored guides
  • After the interview, paste your notes back into MossAIc and ask it to extract key themes

Remember

Always remove confidential details before prompting. Review every AI output — you own the final deliverable.
