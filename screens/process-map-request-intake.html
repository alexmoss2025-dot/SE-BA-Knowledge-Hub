Business Request Intake to Prioritization

How new business requests move from submission through evaluation, scoring, and prioritization committee review to final placement on the roadmap or backlog.

Back to Library

Overview

This map shows the end-to-end flow from when a business request is submitted by a stakeholder through the evaluation, scoring, and prioritization steps that place it into the active roadmap, backlog, or parking lot. The process ensures every request receives a fair, transparent assessment and that high-value, high-urgency work surfaces to the top.

Use this map when onboarding new stakeholders to the intake process, explaining why requests may be deferred, or designing improvements to the prioritization workflow.

Key Roles

Requestor / Sponsor

Submits the request, clarifies the business problem, and commits to funding and championing the work if approved.

Business Analyst

Logs the request, runs discovery, drafts the intake summary, and scores value/effort/risk.

BA Practice Lead

Reviews scoring, assigns analysts, and facilitates the prioritization committee.

Prioritization Committee

Regional/BU leaders who make the final roadmap vs. backlog vs. defer decision based on portfolio fit and capacity.

PMO

Registers approved initiatives that meet the project threshold and links them to the portfolio tracker.

Enterprise Systems

Maintains the intake form, portfolio tracker, and automated notifications to stakeholders.
